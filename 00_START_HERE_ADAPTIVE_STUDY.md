## 2. Three separate ways to learn the SAME ENTIRE COURSE

### ESSENTIEL — dense, complete exam-critical recall, NOT a simplistic short summary

Select ALL facts necessary to correctly answer the exam from the WHOLE course, including final pages. Preserve all required definitions, morphology, every examinable dimension/colour, stages, molecule names, relationships, steps, labels, numerical values, dates, bold original SHS labels, authors, and full required board membership. Be concise, precise and high-density. Prefer readable labelled tables, ordered lists/boards, clean equations. No arbitrary 30-item cap, no first-two-pages bias, and no loss of important details merely to shorten a chapter.

### COMPLET — exhaustive, rewritten and structured FC, NOT merely a PDF viewer

Rewrite 100% of the original FC's academic content in clear, logical text, including all original pages, diagrams, legends, tables, labels, numbers, molecules, molecular interactions, structures, units, exceptional cases and advanced details. Explain each section precisely without paraphrase-induced loss. Preserve original PDF pages with legible zoom as cross-check, but original pages are NEVER an acceptable substitute for missing full rewritten text. Clearly mark an unfinished section INCOMPLETE. No irrelevant prose about the PDF or "la FC présente/associe", no repeated formula, and no unrelated filler.

### COMPRENDRE — advanced mechanistic explanation (AT LEAST full-source technical depth)

This is NOT remedial teaching. The user learns quickly and wants rigorous causal understanding to deduce exam answers instead of memorizing. Start from ALL source facts, then explain exact molecular structures, partners and affinities; spatial organization; anatomical compartment; relevant physico-chemical mechanisms; where and when regulation acts; activation/inhibition; time sequence; consequences of disruptions; structure-function dependencies; histological recognition and quantitative implications. Do not omit any source molecule, dimension or interaction. Give a coherent connected explanation, not a padded summary or generic bullet list. Delineate:
- FC EXIGIBLE: every fact attested in the user's original FC with source page.
- MÉCANISME EXPLICATIF: the reasoned process connecting those facts, with known and uncertain relationships distinguished.
- APPROFONDISSEMENT HORS FC: deeper sourced scientific knowledge, plainly separate from exam-required FC wording.

Example minimum for fertilization: head size; flagellum length; acrosome layers, chromatin, proximal/distal centrioles; intermediate piece OF THE SPERMATOZOON, mitochondrial sheath and axoneme; principal/terminal pieces; meiosis and sperm maturation; capacitation and membrane cholesterol; ZP1/2/3, galactosyltransferase, sp95, hyaluronidase, acrosin; IZUMO1/JUNO/CD9 membrane localizations; PLCζ, IP3, Ca²⁺ and cortical granules; pronuclei. Explain every relevant interaction and consequence. For SHS, develop rigorous social, economic, legal and historical reasoning **while preserving the original bold text, exact board names, full rows and membership**.

Physics/biochemistry: use correct displayed fractions and SI units with clear variable meanings, application conditions and mechanisms; no duplicated formula display or unnecessary derivations. All subjects adapt their structure to their material.

## 3. FLASHCARDS — high volume, SHORT and atomic

ONE independently answerable fact per card (or one tightly related short list explicitly required by the FC). Concise, specific, natural French front and equally short exact answer. Name the relevant structure/gamete/tissue in the question; avoid ambiguous referents. Source provenance is hidden metadata, never content on the answer face. Generate as many as needed from every source fact and the last pages; do NOT cap arbitrarily or artificially inflate counts. Preserve old records and only activate reviewed cards.
- GOOD: "Que contient la pièce intermédiaire du spermatozoïde ?" → "L’axonème et le manchon mitochondrial."
- GOOD: "Quelle glycoprotéine participe à la liaison secondaire à la zone pellucide selon la FC ?" → "ZP2."
- GOOD: "Quelle est la longueur du flagelle du spermatozoïde selon la FC ?" → "60 µm."
- BAD: "Quels éléments définissent le chapitre... ?", "Que retenir de...", "La FC associe...", "Restitue tout le cours", 300-word answer, irrelevant "0,2 mm = 200 µm" conversion.
- SHS requires separate cards for every bold term and list row, board-title membership, full board recall when exam-required, and atomic component details. Do not merge whole tables into a single ordinary flashcard.
- Card fields: stable ID, chapter/subject, specific source fact IDs, source-page metadata, natural front, atomic back, version, difficulty/progress and explicit review status. Reject duplicates, vague labels, unexplained acronyms, wrong referents and malformed Q/A. Keep "À revoir / Difficile / Bien / Facile" with working scheduling and progress.

- NEW PRACTICE mode: WRITE new questions with the realistic difficulty and A–E format shown in the corrected annales/QE. - Each new question stores course ID, linked fact IDs, reference annal style, format (QCM_AE/QROC), exact prompt/options, correct set, five rationale texts or full QROC expected points, page provenance, scoring rules and reviewed status. No invented source citations or answer keys.
