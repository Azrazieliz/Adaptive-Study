# ADAPTIVE STUDY — USER-ORDERED BUILD FREEZE

**Priority: P0, effective 2026-11-04.** This directive overrides the previous practice of building intermediate APKs.

## Exact instruction

> Don't build any app until everything was restructured entirely.

**No new Android APK build, Gradle assemble, signing, ADB installation, temporary distribution link, repackaging or midway release** until the entire Adaptive Study academic corpus has been fully restructured, reviewed and its work recorded in a verifiable coverage report. This includes the 76 currently registered chapters and any subsequently supplied FCs. Reuse and preserve the last working installed app and existing assets. Existing v3.34.0 is an old snapshot, **not** a completed academic deliverable. Do not call it complete or attempt to distribute it as the new version.

## Work permitted during the freeze

Proceed with source reading, manual review of all FC PDF pages and figures, extraction of the exact source fact ledger, content editing, scientific research with clearly separated external knowledge, restructuring **Essentiel / Complet / Comprendre**, fixing malformed flashcards and pre-existing exam questions, source-linked corrections and comprehensible subject-specific boards/mind maps. Change the working data and documentation, and commit source/data changes to GitHub (never upload private FC PDFs or copyrighted exam scans to public repositories). Source edits are permitted. Packaging, compiling and installation are not.

## Completion gate — all must pass before requesting release authorization

1. Each of the 76+ registered chapters has its *correct independent or explicitly composite* FC source inventory, and all source pages, bold terms, illustrations, legends, scientific numbers, tables, board membership and replacement/crossed-out pages were reviewed. Coverage is assessed against **distinct source images/documents**, not inflated composite-page counts.
2. Every examinable source statement and page element is represented in an auditable source fact ledger with a source-page location and exact source wording. Unresolved source images, ambiguous facts, or mistaken exam claims must be explicitly resolved.
3. **Essentiel:** all exam-critical details throughout the full document; no arbitrary word/fact cap. **Complet:** scientifically accurate full textual restatement of all source academic details, not PDF-only. **Comprendre:** at least the same facts PLUS sufficiently technical molecular, structural, causal and quantitative explanations; additional science labeled as outside FC.
4. **SHS:** every original table/board and each entry, original bold heading, corresponding authors/dates and conceptual relations; full board membership recall. **Biology/histology:** exact cell/organ sizes, colour/staining, molecules, compartments, ligands/receptors and interactions, processes, stages and consequences. **Physics:** correctly formatted mathematical fractions, laws, units and assumptions; no redundant formulas or irrelevant derivations.
5. **Flashcards:** concise specific single-fact Q/A (e.g. *Que contient la pièce intermédiaire du spermatozoïde ?*) across every examinable atomic fact; incorrect, vague, overlong and duplicate cards corrected or quarantined with unchanged progress IDs where feasible.
6. **Questions:** preserve only the approved newly written A–E/QROC practice set in the student app; audit or revise previously poor questions against available annales and their actual corrections, with fully justified answer options. **No original annales/QE PDF library** must be returned to the app; source documents may remain archived outside it. No fabrication where no exam source exists.
7. **Mind maps/boards:** retain existing images, and provide the subject-appropriate legible boards, diagrams or formula sheets from the supplied FCs. These are lower priority than actual scientific lesson completeness, but are not deleted.
8. Audit the source-to-output coverage with a real manifest: per chapter, per unique FC source page, missing academic elements, and mode-specific editorial verification. **Do not mark complete just because a section is indexed or an image is included.** All remaining academic gaps must be zero and every chapter must be editorially certified before the build freeze can be lifted.

Do not bypass this freeze with an "intermediate", "test", "urgent", or "automatic" build. Do not claim all 76 chapters complete because the reading component exists for all 76. Do not ask the user to manually review a test APK before the content is completely restructured; they explicitly withheld authorization to build until then.
